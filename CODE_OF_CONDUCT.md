# Community Code of Conduct

Forma's community should be a practical, respectful place to build software together. Participants
must treat others with consideration across issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and
other project spaces.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person presenting them.
- Give technical criticism with enough context to be actionable.
- Respect differences in experience, identity, language, and communication style.
- Stop unwanted contact or attention when asked.
- Protect private information and report security-sensitive material confidentially.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted. Publishing another person's private
communication or identity without permission is also not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `igor@zigrok.com`. Include relevant links or screenshots and
the outcome needed to feel safe participating. Do not open a public issue about another person's
conduct.

Project maintainers will acknowledge a report within five business days, limit details to people
needed to assess it, and avoid conflicts of interest when selecting a reviewer. Responses may include
a private warning, content removal, temporary participation limits, or a permanent ban. Retaliation
against a reporter or witness is itself a violation.

This policy applies whenever someone represents Forma or Zigrok in a project-related space.
Maintainers remain accountable to the same standard and may be recused from a report concerning
them.
